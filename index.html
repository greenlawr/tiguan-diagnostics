  1. Plug the adapter into the socket under the dashboard, just above the pedals.
  2. Switch the ignition on. The engine can be off or running.
  3. Tap the button below and choose your adapter from the list.

        Security & backup
        
                                Your data is encrypted on this phone with your passphrase. It never leaves the device. There is no cloud copy, so keep a backup and don't forget your passphrase — it can't be recovered.
                                
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
                                Stops anyone guessing your passphrase. Off unless you switch it on. Fingerprint / Face ID tries don't count. Save a backup first — private things only come back from a backup.

                                How Evergreen works
                                

                                
  The basics
  
                                Home shows what needs attention soon, this week's family calendar, and a tile for every area. Life holds everything else: calendar, shopping, deliveries, documents, utilities and meters, money, home, health, loyalty cards, and the shared bills and tasks.
                                
  
                                Everything you add is encrypted on your phone. Dates you add (MOT, renewals, document expiry, check-ups) appear on Home as they get close.
                                

                                


                                
  Sharing with your partner
  
                                Pair once under Life → Sharing & sync: one phone taps Create a shared space, the other taps Join with a code and enters the same code, with the same server address.
                                
  
                                Shared between you: the family calendar, shopping list (with photos), to-do list, family details, deliveries, meter readings and suppliers, and shared bills. Kept private to each phone: documents, health, money, loyalty cards, car and everything else.
                                
  
                                It syncs when you open the app, when you switch back to it, every minute while it's open, and straight after you change something. It can't sync while the app is closed — you'll get the latest the moment you open it.
                                
  
                                The code is the key to your shared space. Only share it with your partner.
                                

                                


                                
  Family calendar & the morning check
  
                                Calendar: each of you connects your own Google Calendar under Life → Family calendar (it shows you where to find your calendar's secret address). Your phone then keeps your events up to date by itself, and your partner sees them too. Home shows what's on today, with the rest of the week one tap away.
                                
  
                                Add words like "Foyer" to Don't share events containing to keep work events off the family calendar. You can also add a one-off event by hand.
                                
  
                                Morning check: each morning Claude checks your Gmail for parcels and sends you one link — tap it and they drop in on both phones. Nothing is ever added twice.
                                

                                


                                
  Deliveries
  
                                Parcels arrive with the morning link, or add one under Life → Deliveries. Tap Track parcel to open the courier's page, and Arrived when it's here — it disappears from both phones.
                                

                                


                                
  Meter readings
  
                                Under Life → Utilities there's a panel for electricity (kWh), gas (m³) and water (m³). Type the number on the meter and tap Add. After two readings it shows how much you use a day. Either of you can add one.
                                

                                


                                
  Photos on the shopping list
  
                                Tap 📷 next to any item to take or choose a photo — handy for "this exact brand". Tap the thumbnail to see it full size or remove it. Photos sync to your partner and are deleted when the item is.
                                

                                


                                
  Documents & scanning
  
                                Under Life → Documents, add passports, licences, insurance and so on with their expiry dates to get reminders. You can attach a photo, and scanning a passport fills in the details for you. Photos are encrypted on your phone.
                                

                                


                                
  Installing on another phone
  
                                Open greenlawr.github.io/tiguan-diagnostics in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android). iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: ⋮ → Install app. Always open it from the home-screen icon, then set a passphrase and pair under Sharing & sync.
                                

                                


                                
  If a phone is lost or stolen
  
                                Your data is encrypted on the phone, so it can't be read without the passphrase. For extra peace of mind, either of you can lock a lost phone out from the other phone: Life → Sharing & sync → If a phone is lost → Report lost, then enter the lost phone owner's Evergreen passphrase.
                                
  
                                That cuts it off from your shared lists, calendar and bills straight away, deletes the old copy from your server, and gives you a new sharing code. The next time Evergreen is opened on the lost phone with signal — even just the lock screen — it erases itself.
                                
  
                                Optional: Help → Security & backup → Erase after 10 wrong passphrase attempts stops anyone guessing your passphrase, even without signal.
                                
  
                                Turn on your phone's own remote erase too — it can lock or wipe the whole phone, not just Evergreen:
                                
  
                                  
    
                                • Android: Settings → Google → Find My Device → On (and turn on Store recent location). If the phone is lost, go to google.com/android/find on any device and choose Factory reset device.
                                  • 
    
                                • iPhone: Settings → your name → Find My → Find My iPhone → On (and Send Last Location). If the phone is lost, go to icloud.com/find and choose Erase This Device.
                                  • 
  
                                
  
                                Do the Evergreen "Report lost" step as well: a phone that's switched off can't be erased remotely until it reconnects, but the moment Evergreen is opened it checks in.
                                

                                


                                
  Your privacy
  
                                Your data is encrypted with your passphrase and stays on your phone. The shared items go through your own sync server, which only ever sees scrambled data it can't read. There's no account, and a forgotten passphrase can't be recovered by anyone, so keep a backup.
                                

                                

      
                                Your car
                                

                                
  Car details & MOT history (setting up)
  
                                Evergreen can fill in your car's details, tax and MOT dates, and the full MOT history (every pass, fail and advisory) from the official government records. You need two free keys, once:
                                
  
                                  
    
                                1. DVLA Vehicle Enquiry key (make, colour, tax and MOT dates): apply at register-for-ves.driver-vehicle-licensing.api.gov.uk. They email you an API key.
                                  2. 
    
                                2. DVSA MOT History (every MOT result): register at documentation.history.mot.api.gov.uk. They email a client ID, client secret, token URL and API key.
                                  3. 
    
                                3. In Cloudflare (your personal account): evergreen-sync → Settings → Variables and Secrets → Add, choose type Secret for each:
      DVLA_API_KEY, MOT_CLIENT_ID, MOT_CLIENT_SECRET, MOT_API_KEY, MOT_TOKEN_URL.
                                  4. 
    
                                4. On the Car tab, type your registration — the details fill in by themselves.
                                  5. 
  
                                
  
                                The keys stay on your server, never on the phones. Either key works on its own. DVSA cancels an MOT key that isn't used for 90 days — Evergreen refreshes weekly, which keeps it active.
                                

                                


                                
        Car check: getting started
        
                                You need a small Bluetooth adapter that plugs into the car's diagnostic socket. It must say Bluetooth LE, BLE or Bluetooth 4.0 on the listing.
                                
        
                                  
          
                                • OBDLink CX: the most reliable choice.
                                  • 
          
                                • Vgate iCar Pro BLE 4.0: a good cheaper option.
                                  • 
        
                                
        
                                The socket is under the dashboard on the driver's side, above the pedals. The adapter only fits one way round. You can leave it plugged in for a check, but unplug it if the car will be parked for days.
                                
      
                                


                                
        What the colours mean
        
                                  
          
                                • All good Nothing to do.
                                  • 
          
                                • Keep an eye on it Worth knowing, no action needed now.
                                  • 
          
                                • Get it checked OK to drive, but book a garage visit soon.
                                  • 
          
                                • Act now Could cause damage or leave you stranded.
                                  • 
        
                                
        
                                This app gives guidance, not a guarantee. If the car feels unsafe, or a red warning shows on the dashboard, stop and get help regardless of what the app says.
                                
      
                                


                                
        Google Maps on the car's screen
        
                                You do not need this app or the adapter for that. Your Tiguan can show Google Maps from your phone using Android Auto, which VW calls App-Connect. It is free and always has up-to-date maps and live traffic.
                                
        
                                  
          
                                1. Use a good quality USB cable (the one that came with your phone is best).
                                  2. 
          
                                2. Plug the phone into the USB socket in the storage tray in front of the gear lever.
                                  3. 
          
                                3. On the car's screen press App or Menu, then App-Connect, then Android Auto.
                                  4. 
          
                                4. Accept the prompts on the phone. Google Maps now appears on the car's screen.
                                  5. 
        
                                
        
                                If App-Connect is missing or asks for an activation code, your car has the feature fitted but switched off. A VW dealer or independent VW specialist can switch it on for a one-off fee. That is still usually cheaper than paying for map updates, and you only pay once.
                                
        
                                Want it without a cable? Once wired Android Auto works, a small "wireless Android Auto adapter" that plugs into the same USB socket makes it connect automatically.
                                
      
                                


                                
        Android Auto isn't working — why?
        
                                This reads your car's touchscreen unit and works out which situation you are in. It cannot switch Android Auto on — that needs a signed activation code for your exact unit that only a dealer or specialist coder can supply — but it tells you whether that is even what you need, so you do not pay for the wrong thing.
                                
        
        
                                
        
                                
      
                                


                                
        Updating the car's own maps and software
        
                                Built-in maps. If your car has the "Discover Media" navigation system, VW has offered map updates as a free download from its website, which you copy onto the car's SD card yourself. Search for "Volkswagen map update Discover Media" on VW's official site for your country before paying anyone.
                                
        
                                The car's computers. This app shows the software version of each computer in your car (open a system under "Systems checked"). It deliberately cannot change that software: updates are locked to VW's own dealer equipment, and attempting one with a small Bluetooth adapter can permanently break the part. If a dealer says an update is available, the versions shown here let you check it was really applied.
                                
      
                                


                                
        Turn off the engine warning light
        
                                This wipes the engine's stored faults and switches the warning light off. Only do it after a repair, or to see whether a one-off fault comes back.
                                
        
                                  
          
                                • It does not fix anything. If the problem is still there the light will return.
                                  • 
          
                                • The car then needs a few days of normal driving to re-run its self-tests. It may be turned away from an MOT emissions test until it has.
                                  • 
          
                                • Do it with the ignition on and the engine off.
                                  • 
        
                                
        
        
                                
      
                                


                                
        Words you might see
        
                                
          
                                Fault code
                                A short reference like P0299 that the car stores when it notices a problem. Garages use these to look up what went wrong.
                                
          
                                Engine warning light
                                The amber engine-shaped light on the dashboard. Steady means "get it checked"; flashing means "stop when safe".
                                
          
                                Limp mode
                                When the car deliberately limits its power to protect itself after detecting a fault.
                                
          
                                Emissions self-tests
                                Checks the car runs on itself while you drive. They restart from scratch after faults are cleared or the battery is disconnected.
                                
          
                                VIN
                                Your car's unique 17-character identity number.
                                
          
                                Turbo
                                A pump driven by the exhaust that pushes extra air into the engine for more power.
